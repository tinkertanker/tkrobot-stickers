# Style DNA

## One line

Pure white, minimal, hand-drawn, generous whitespace, restrained, whimsical,
product-sketch feeling, handwritten English labels, structurally clear but
never a manual.

Like someone who builds AI tools and teaches coding sketched an explanation on
a blank sheet — quickly, precisely, and slightly absurdly.

## Character

T Krobot is the sticker mascot in thin-line form. Light grey tube limbs, black
trapezoid head and body, empty round white glasses, red chest diamond, hands
that are a black palm blob with three grey rectangular finger tabs plus a
thumb: open, point, thumbs-up (palm-closed), or closed at rest. See
`tkrobot-sketch-mode.md` and the lock sheets. If the robot is an all-black
stick figure, or the hands are gloves or wrapped around a handle, the page
is wrong even when the layout is right.

## Must

- 16:9 landscape body illustration.
- Pure white background: no cream, warm grey, paper texture, gradient, shadow,
  noise, or vintage-paper feel.
- Black hand-drawn line art: thin lines, slight wobble, not mechanical, not
  vector, not heavy sticker outlines.
- Generous whitespace: subject occupies ~40–60% of the canvas; at least 35%
  blank; ideally one whole quiet region.
- Sparse handwritten English annotations: at most 5–8, each 1–5 words,
  British spelling.
- One image expresses one core action, structure, state, or metaphor.
- The structure expresses itself; never write the structure-type name on the image.

## Colours

- **Black** — line art, T Krobot's body, frames, structures, main objects.
- **Red** (#FB0101 family) — key warnings, problems, emotional peaks, results.
  T Krobot's chest diamond is always this red, and is often the only solid red.
- **Orange** (#FCA403 family) — the main flow: paths, arrows, movement A→B,
  automation direction.
- **Teal** (#2E7D74 family) — secondary notes, inner/system state, the
  AI-assistant voice, second-layer explanation.

Teal is optional per image. Restraint beats coverage.

## Never

No commercial illustration. No PPT infographics. No formal flowcharts. No
course slides. No cute cartoon posters. No children's-book art. No complex
architecture diagrams. No polished flat illustration. No techy UI. No real app
screenshots. No busy backgrounds, gradients, shadows, textures. No explaining
every node. No corner titles like "Workflow", "Roadmap", "Common pitfalls".

## Taste

Whimsical, inventive, interesting, clean, unexpected.
Not cute, not childish, not busy, not stiff.
