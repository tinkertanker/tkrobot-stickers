# T Krobot in sketch mode

The canonical T Krobot (see `../tkrobot-sticker-generation/references/style-guide.md`)
is a thick-outline flat sticker character. Body illustrations use **sketch
mode**: the same character drawn as thin, slightly wobbly pen line art.

## Invariants that survive sketch mode

- Softened trapezoid head, top ~90% as wide as the bottom; softened trapezoid
  body; no neck.
- Large round white glasses with black rims — wide and round, allowed to
  overhang the head sides. White lens fill is what keeps the face readable on
  a solid-black head. No pupils by default.
- Flat red diamond on the chest — usually the only solid red in the drawing.
- Thin limbs; hands read as simple mitten-or-tab shapes at sketch scale
  (exact three-finger anatomy relaxes when the character is small).
- Oversized oval feet. No mouth, ever. Blank, serious, deadpan expression.

## What relaxes

- Line weight: thin wobbly pen line replaces the thick sticker outline.
- Body fill: solid black ink fill is preferred (reads like the canon and
  anchors the page); loose hatching acceptable at very small sizes.
- Proportions may squash/stretch slightly with the action — carrying,
  cranking, falling — as long as head/glasses/diamond read.

## Character in the scene

T Krobot is a serious, deadpan worker doing an absurd-but-coherent job. It
must perform the image's core action: stuck inside the machine, pulling the
wrong cable, guarding the gate, hauling the crate, weighing the ideas,
patching the pipe, feeding pages into a strange device.

Multiple T Krobots are allowed (2–3 max) when the structure needs stations —
each doing one job. Never a crowd, never a mascot lineup.

## Never in sketch mode

- No mouth, no eyebrows, no blush, no cute face.
- No reverting to thick sticker outlines or flat-colour sticker shading.
- No other recurring characters. Humans only as rare faceless extras.
